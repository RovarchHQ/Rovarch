# AI usage policy

Rovarch is developed with AI assistance. The rules below keep that
assistance explicit and accountable.

- A person opens every pull request. AI agents do not open, merge, or
  automate pull requests on their own.
- The person who opens the pull request is the author of every change in
  it and is responsible for all of them.
- Commits are authored by a person, not by a tool. AI generators are not
  listed as authors or co-authors on a commit.
- A person reads and understands the diff before it is opened. Generated
  output that has not been reviewed must not be merged.
- A person writes the pull request description. Writing it is the
  self-review. Translation for a non-native speaker is the one exception.
